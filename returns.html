Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Each Koncrete Art Kitchen piece is designed and made in-house, and many are personalised for you. We want you to be happy with your order, and we stand behind the quality of our work. This policy explains how returns, replacements and refunds work.

2. Made-to-order and personalised pieces

Because most pieces are made to order and/or personalised with your name or design, they cannot be returned or exchanged for a change of mind. If you have any doubt before ordering — size, layout, spelling or finish — please confirm the details with us on WhatsApp before we begin production.

3. Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Please inspect your order as soon as it arrives. If a piece arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with:

  • Your order details (name and phone number used at checkout);
  • A short description of the issue;
  • Clear photos or a short video showing the piece and its packaging.

Once we verify the issue, we will arrange a repair, a replacement, or a refund as appropriate. In genuine cases of damage or defect, we cover the cost of the return and the replacement.

4. Cancellations

You may cancel free of charge before production of your piece has started. Once production has begun, cancellation may not be possible, especially for personalised pieces. To cancel, message us on WhatsApp as early as possible.

5. Lost or undelivered orders

If your order is delayed, lost or not delivered, contact us and we will coordinate with the courier to trace and resolve it. Where an order cannot be delivered to you, we will arrange a replacement or a refund.

6. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it is issued to the original payment method used at checkout. Refunds are usually processed within 5–7 working days after approval, though the time for the amount to appear in your account can vary depending on your bank or UPI app.

7. Non-returnable situations

  • Change of mind after production has started;
  • Damage caused after delivery through mishandling or improper installation;
  • Personalisation details that were confirmed by you but later changed;
  • Requests made more than 48 hours after delivery.

8. How to raise a request

Message us on WhatsApp with your order details and photos, or use the chat assistant on this website. We will respond as quickly as possible and guide you through the next steps.
