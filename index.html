01 — About

A studio that makes its own materials, models, and molds.

Koncrete Art Kitchen is a design-led product development and manufacturing studio founded in 2024. We create premium decorative and architectural pieces built on gypsum-based systems and acrylic polymer technology, shaped by a workshop culture of constant experimentation.

Our first year was defined by research rather than output. We tested materials, refined formulations, and rebuilt our processes in pursuit of a single, demanding standard: a consistently smooth, refined surface, free of the bubbles that compromise cast gypsum products. Through sustained experimentation and process development, we engineered and optimized an in-house method that yields a substantially bubble-free finish, with the depth and consistency our work demands.

What distinguishes the studio is how much of the work stays within our own walls. We develop our own 3D product models, prepare them for manufacture, produce high-quality 3D prints, carry out detailed surface finishing, and build custom silicone molds for casting. Raw and specialized materials are sourced from trusted external suppliers, while design development, prototyping, model finishing, mold making, and casting remain under our direct supervision — allowing precise tolerances, confident customization, and quality control at every step.

03 — Craft

Specified like joinery. Lit like a lamp.

Standard diameter is 450 mm. Total thickness 20 mm. Relief sits 3–5 mm proud; the body is 15–18 mm; a 10–15 mm wall gap lets the COB strip throw a warm ring. Lettering is raised. Names set in Cinzel; secondary lines in a humanist sans.

Wave, drape, and custom textures are modelled in-house before the pour. Each master is printed, hand-finished, and used to develop a custom silicone mold. Finishes are not paint chips — they are the mix: gypsum and acrylic polymer in natural concrete, sandstone, taupe, charcoal, and ivory.

From the name to the wall.

  1. 01

    Brief

  2. 02

    Model

    Relief and typography developed as a 3D model in-house.

  3. 03

    Print

    High-quality 3D print, then detailed surface finishing by hand.

  4. 04

    Mold

    A custom silicone mold is developed from the finished master.

  5. 05

    Cast

    Gypsum and acrylic polymer cast, controlled for a refined surface.

  6. 06

    Finish & Light
