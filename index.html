01 — About

A studio that models, prototypes, and manufactures in-house.

Koncrete Art Kitchen is a design-led product development and manufacturing studio founded in 2024. We create premium decorative and architectural pieces, shaped by a workshop culture of constant experimentation.

Our first year was defined by research rather than output. We tested, refined, and rebuilt our processes in pursuit of a single, demanding standard: a consistently smooth, refined surface, free of the bubbles that compromise cast products. Through sustained experimentation and process development, we engineered and optimized an in-house method that yields a substantially bubble-free finish, with the depth and consistency our work demands.

What distinguishes the studio is how much of the work stays within our own walls. We develop our own 3D product models, prepare them for manufacture, produce high-quality 3D prints, carry out detailed surface finishing, and build custom silicone molds for casting. Raw and specialized materials are sourced from trusted external suppliers, while design development, prototyping, model finishing, mold making, and casting remain under our direct supervision — allowing precise tolerances, confident customization, and quality control at every step.

03 — Craft

Made to a standard, not a template.

Every piece is developed from a drawing and built to the millimetre. Proportion, relief, and line are resolved before production begins, so the finished work matches the intent — not the other way round.

Textures are modelled in-house. Each master is printed, hand-finished, and used to develop a custom silicone mould. From print to finish, the same hands carry the work, giving dimensional consistency and a refined, repeatable surface.

The studio is set up for customisation: a new name, a new proportion, a new texture. Because development and manufacturing sit under one roof, we can take a piece from concept to completion with control at every step.
