Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This Shipping Policy explains how we process, pack and deliver Koncrete Art Kitchen orders. Because many of our pieces are made to order, please allow for production time in addition to delivery time.

2. Order processing and production

Once your order and payment are confirmed, we begin production. Made-to-order and personalised pieces take longer than ready pieces. We will share an estimated production and dispatch timeline with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

3. Shipping coverage

We ship across India through trusted courier partners. If you are outside India, please contact us on WhatsApp before ordering so we can confirm whether delivery is possible and the applicable charges.

4. Shipping charges

Shipping charges depend on your delivery location, the size and weight of the piece, and the courier. The exact shipping charge is confirmed with you on WhatsApp before dispatch, so there are no surprises.

5. Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes a few working days depending on your location and the courier. Remote areas may take longer. Delivery timelines are estimates and are not guaranteed, as courier delays can happen.

6. Tracking

Where the courier provides tracking, we share the tracking details with you on WhatsApp so you can follow your shipment.

7. Packaging

Each piece is packed carefully with protective material to help it reach you safely. We recommend keeping the packaging until you have inspected the piece.

8. Delays

Production or delivery may be delayed by factors beyond our control, such as weather, courier issues or public holidays. We will keep you informed and do our best to resolve any delay quickly.

9. Damaged, lost or undelivered shipments

Please inspect your order when it arrives. If it is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. If a shipment is lost or undelivered, contact us and we will coordinate with the courier to trace and resolve it. See our Return & Refund Policy for details.

10. Address changes

If you need to change your delivery address, message us on WhatsApp as early as possible. Once an order has been dispatched, we may not be able to change the address.

11. Contact us

For any shipping question, message us on WhatsApp or use the chat assistant on this website.
