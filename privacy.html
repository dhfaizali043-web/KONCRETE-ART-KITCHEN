Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Koncrete Art Kitchen ("we", "us", "our") is a design-led studio that sells made-in-house pieces through this website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using this website or placing an order, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address and order note;
  • Account details (optional): if you choose to create an account, your email address and a password;
  • Order history: the items, amounts and payment method for your orders;
  • Communications: messages you send us on WhatsApp or through the chat assistant;
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends, such as pages visited, and items saved in your cart.

We do not collect or store your card number, UPI PIN, CVV or banking passwords.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, make, pack and deliver your order;
  • To confirm details and answer your questions on WhatsApp or chat;
  • To provide order history if you create an account;
  • To send updates about your order;
  • To improve our products, website and service;
  • To meet legal and accounting requirements.

4. Accounts and where data is stored

Creating an account is optional — you can check out as a guest. If you create an account, your email, name, phone, address and order history are stored securely with our authentication and database service provider. Each signed-in customer can only see their own account and orders.

5. Payments

Payments made by card or netbanking are processed by our third-party payment provider. Payments by UPI or bank transfer are made directly through your own app or bank. We only receive a confirmation of payment and the transaction reference; we never see or store your full card or banking credentials.

6. Cookies and local storage

This website uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart and, if you sign in, your session. We do not use this to track you across other websites. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings.

7. Sharing your information

We share information only where needed to fulfil your order — for example with courier partners for delivery and with payment providers for transactions. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Data security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can place an order as a guest without creating an account;
  • You can log out of your account at any time;
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information;
  • You can ask us to stop sending order updates (other than essential order messages).

10. Children

Our website is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

12. Contact us

For any privacy question or request, message us on WhatsApp or use the chat assistant on this website, and we will respond as quickly as possible.
