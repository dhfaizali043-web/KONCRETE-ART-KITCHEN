Shop / Products

Edit any field, add or remove products, and upload product images from your device. Uploaded images are stored in the repository under public/brand/products. Click Save & publish at the bottom to make changes live (about one minute).

Orders

Sign in with an owner email (set in Customer accounts) to see every order and update its status. Customers only ever see their own orders.

Payments

These details are shown to customers at checkout. Fill in whichever methods you accept; leave the rest blank to hide them.

Chat assistant

The chat bubble shown on the website answers customer questions using your products, prices, payment details and the knowledge base below. One entry per line in the format Question | Answer.

Customer accounts (login)

Turn on customer login and order history. This uses Firebase (Google) — it has a free plan. Create a project at console.firebase.google.com, add a Web app, and paste its config values below. Then enable the sign-in methods you want (Email/Password and/or Google) under Authentication, and create a Firestore database under Firestore Database. Login is optional for customers — guests can still checkout without an account.

These config values are public by design (they only identify your project).
